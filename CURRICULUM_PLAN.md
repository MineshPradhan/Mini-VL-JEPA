# Mini VL-JEPA — Caption Quality Curriculum Plan

**Goal (as directed by guide):** make caption generation work properly, starting with low-complexity videos, then high, then difficult — English first, multilingual after.

## Why captions currently look random (diagnosis)

1. **Train/inference embedding gap.** The decoder was trained to decode *ground-truth* LaBSE caption embeddings, but at inference it receives the *predictor's* output, which is only partially aligned (≈19.7% R@1). The decoder has never seen inputs from that region of the space.
2. **Single-vector bottleneck.** One 768-d sentence embedding compresses the whole video; decoding fluent text from one point is inherently hard.
3. **Decoding configuration.** Sampling or greedy decoding from a weak conditioning signal amplifies the problem; beam search with repetition control is required.

**Fixes:** (a) CapDec-style noise injection — train the decoder on GT embeddings + Gaussian noise matched to the predictor's measured error, then fine-tune on real predicted embeddings; (b) project the embedding into 16 pseudo-encoder tokens instead of one; (c) beam-search decoding. Decoder training is text-only (no video), so iteration is fast on the RTX 3050.

## Complexity tiers (guide's low / high / difficult)

Each video is scored from its 20 captions: **caption agreement** (mean pairwise LaBSE cosine — high agreement means the content is simple and unambiguous), **caption length**, and **vocabulary rarity** (optionally + visual motion). Terciles of the combined score give **easy / medium / hard** tiers.

## Stages

| Stage | Data | Language | Action | Gate to pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | — | EN | Diagnose: oracle decode from GT embeddings; measure cos(pred, GT) | Oracle captions fluent + on-topic |
| 1 | easy tier | EN | Train decoder with noise injection; then fine-tune with 50% real predicted embeddings | Predicted-embedding captions on easy videos are coherent; LaBSE-sim within ~85% of oracle |
| 2 | easy + medium | EN | Continue training (init from Stage 1) | Medium-tier captions coherent; easy tier does not regress |
| 3 | all tiers | EN | Continue training (init from Stage 2) | Best-effort on hard tier; report per-tier metrics honestly |
| 4 | all tiers | EN + HI | Reintroduce Hindi / multilingual decoding | Hindi quality on easy tier comparable to Stage 1 English |

## Metrics (reported per tier, validation split)

BLEU-4 (multi-reference), ROUGE-L (best over references), **LaBSE-similarity** of the generated caption vs the closest reference — semantic adequacy in the project's own embedding space. Oracle vs predicted results are always reported side by side so decoder quality and predictor quality are separated.

## Deliverables per stage

`results/<stage>_oracle.md` and `results/<stage>_pred.md` — metric tables + qualitative examples per tier, generated by `eval_captions.py`, suitable for review meetings.
